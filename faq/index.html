Questions & Answers

Where to park, what a haircut costs, how long it takes, curly hair, deposits and how to book.

Where are you located?

410 NE 1st Ave, Hallandale Beach, FL 33009, a short drive from Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach and Hollywood.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking across from the salon. Drive in from the railroad side.

How much is a women's haircut?

$140, whatever your hair length or the shape you choose. The price includes the consultation, wash, blow-dry and home-styling tips.

Do you charge more for long or thick hair?

No. Short, shoulder-length, medium and long hair all cost the same, and complex shapes have no extra fee.

How long does a haircut take?

1.5–2 hours, including consultation, dry-hair diagnosis, the cut, wash and blow-dry.

What are your hours?

Thursday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm. Closed Sunday through Wednesday.

Do you cut curly hair?

Yes, it's one of my main specialties. I cut with curl shrinkage and your curl pattern in mind and build the shape for how your hair looks dry, not blown straight.

What haircut is best for fine, thin hair?

Usually a shape with a dense outline, like a blunt bob or a bob with minimal graduation, with layers only where they help. Over-thinning makes fine hair look thinner.

Can I book just a consultation?

Yes. It's $35 for 20 minutes, and the $35 is credited toward your haircut if you book one.

Can you fix a bad haircut from another salon?

Often, yes. Balance, the outline and harsh transitions can usually be corrected in one visit. If too much length or density was removed, I'll give you a step-by-step plan while it grows back.

Do you do color, keratin or extensions?

No, I only do haircuts. If you need color, I can help you book with a colorist.

What if I'm not happy with my haircut?

Within 7 days you can come back for a free small technical adjustment (about 10 minutes). It doesn't include changing the shape or a new haircut.

Do I need a deposit to book?

No deposit is required.

What languages do you speak?

English, Ukrainian and Russian. For other languages I use translation tools.

How do I book?

Online on Vagaro, by WhatsApp message or by Instagram DM. I don't take phone calls; written messages let me answer precisely.

Ready for a haircut that's easy to style at home?

Book online on Vagaro or message me on WhatsApp. $140, 1.5–2 hours, wash and blow-dry included. Thursday–Saturday, 10 am–6 pm, in Hallandale Beach.
