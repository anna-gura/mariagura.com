Booking

How to book

Online on Vagaro (fastest), by WhatsApp message or by Instagram DM. I communicate in writing, not by phone, so I can read your request carefully and answer precisely.

What to send

Your name, last name and contact number. A photo of the cut you like and of your hair as it is now helps me prepare.

Deposit

None. You can book without paying in advance.

7-day touch-up

Within 7 days of your haircut, you can come back for a free, small technical adjustment of about 10 minutes, such as refining a line or one element of the cut. It doesn't cover a new shape, a change of mind about the original request, or a new haircut.
