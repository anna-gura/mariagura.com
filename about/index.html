I'm Maria Gura, a Ukrainian hairstylist with 30 years of experience, specializing in women's haircuts. Before moving to the United States I ran my own hair business in Ukraine, and I also created EdgeLine, a program where I teach other hairstylists modern women's cutting: precision lines, bobs, layered shapes, pixie cuts, bangs and cutting for curly hair.

More about Maria

Teaching means I don't just repeat cuts. I know why each line and angle is there, so I can adapt the same haircut to different textures and head shapes.

I'm an independent hairstylist: I do the consultation and the haircut myself, and all appointments are booked directly with me. I use Davines, Moroccanoil and Vitality's products, chosen by hair type. I speak English, Ukrainian and Russian, and use translation tools for other languages, so you can explain exactly what you want.

"A good haircut isn't a copy of a photo. It's precise work with geometry, hair structure and the shape of the head. I want a woman to look good not only when she leaves the salon, but every day, when she dries her hair herself."— Maria Gura
